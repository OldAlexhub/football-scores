Football Scores Today is free to use and supported by Google AdMob advertising. It has no user accounts and no Old Alex Hub backend — favorites, predictions, matchday plans, and reminders are stored only on your device. Football data is fetched from OpenFootball, football-data.org, and optionally API-Football. AdMob may use device identifiers and approximate location to personalize ads based on your consent choices, which you can review anytime from More → Advertising & Privacy. Full policy: see PRIVACYPOLICY.md / the in-app Privacy Policy screen.
