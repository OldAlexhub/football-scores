# Feature graphic notes — Football Scores Today

Google Play feature graphic size: **1024 × 500 px**, JPEG or 24-bit PNG (no alpha).

## Suggested composition

- Left third: app logo (`assets/logo.png`) on a solid or subtly gradient background using the app's accent green (`#1E7A46` light theme accent, or `#34C77B` dark-theme accent).
- Center: 2–3 stacked match-card mockups showing team initials, a score, and a kickoff time — reuse the real `MatchCard` visual style from the app so the graphic matches what users actually see.
- Right third: the title lockup "Football Scores Today" in a bold sans-serif, with a one-line subtitle: "Scores · Fixtures · Standings · Predictions".

## Do

- Keep all critical text within the safe area (roughly the middle 80% horizontally, middle 90% vertically) — Play Store crops edges on some form factors.
- Use real app screenshots or faithful mockups of real screens.
- Keep contrast high enough to read the title on both light and dark surrounding UI (Play renders this graphic on a plain background, so this is mostly about legibility against your own chosen background).

## Do not

- Do not use the word "Live" in the graphic.
- Do not imply an official partnership with any league, club, or broadcaster.
- Do not include betting odds, betting language, or gambling imagery.
- Do not use the word "MatchPilot" anywhere in the asset.

## Localized variants

Produce at minimum:
- International (English) — "Football Scores Today"
- United States — "Soccer Games Today"
- Arabic (RTL-mirrored layout, logo can stay left or move right depending on how the composition reads better mirrored) — "مباريات ونتائج كرة القدم"
